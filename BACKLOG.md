The three weekly action loops, rebuilt around a "My Team" mode and a new default **Today** tab (daily standing brief): lineup reset as a minimal swap list with points-left-on-bench + ESPN deep links (write API is a hard wall — advice + one-tap deep link is the industry pattern); waiver cards reframed as net gain over your worst starter with add/drop pairing and FAAB bid bands mined from our own `transactions.jsonl`; trades rebuilt as a partner finder (complementary-needs matching, fairness bar, "swings your typical matchup by +X" stakes line). Competitive survey (in-spec appendix) found season-long WNBA tooling has essentially zero competitors. Ships in 5 end-to-end phases. Subsumes the existing "My team mode", "Trade analyzer", "Streamer vs anchor", and "Matchup preview cards" items below — leave them until each phase lands, then fold them in.

- **Trades: OUT players are priced at full value (correctness).** `trades.py` values every player at their healthy `projected_per_game` and filters nothing by availability — 12 of 85 players in currently generated packages are OUT, offered at full price. The lineup checker refuses to *start* an OUT player while the trade generator recommends *acquiring* one; the two contradict each other. P2 mitigated the *display* (injury status now renders next to the price on Today and the Trades tab) but the valuation is still wrong, and it drives the sort order. Fix belongs to P4: discount the projection by expected games missed, or exclude confirmed-OUT players from package search. Reuse `positions.is_confirmed_out()` so the rule stays in one place. Logged in [ISSUES.md](ISSUES.md).

- **Matchup win odds need remaining-schedule modelling.** Spec §5.5 wanted "rough odds" on the matchup frame. Built in P2 and pulled: a normal approximation over `matchup_history` variance only says which team is better *on the season*, so it ignores the live score and rendered "52% odds" next to a 50-point lead. A defensible version needs each side's *remaining* games in the current matchup period, which `state.json` doesn't carry — `schedule.games_by_period()` (added in P1) now makes that computable. Today shows both sides' season averages until then.

- **ESPN cookies stale for 9+ days — run a local full refresh.** Cloud sessions have run in `--social-only` mode continuously since the 2026-07-06 snapshot (at least 8 consecutive refreshes through 2026-07-15); transactions, rosters, and FAAB budget are all still that old. Social/news/rankings stay current, but a real waiver claim, drop, or roster move since 2026-07-06 won't show up anywhere in the UI. Run `/refresh-fantasy-gm` (or `python -m pipeline.refresh`) locally with `.env` cookies set to pull a fresh ESPN snapshot.

Bluesky: public AT Protocol API (`public.api.bsky.app/xrpc/app.bsky.feed.searchPosts`), no auth, per-player queries for top-15 targets. ## Medium

- **`state.json` is 296 KB gzipped — 48% over the spec's 200 KB budget.** Not caused by the GM Console work (lineup_check + current_matchup cost 0.9 KB); the payload was already at 295 KB. `news_by_player` is 64% of the raw bytes (1.3 MB of 2.0 MB), `twitter_posts_by_player` another 13%. The whole page blocks on this fetch before any data renders. Spec §7.4 prescribes the fix: split `state.json` (core: meta/teams/waivers/trades) from `extras.json` (news/social by player, lazy-loaded on tab open with `priority: "low"`). Would cut first-paint payload by roughly 4x. Do this before P5 adds the planner grid.

- **Short-term vs. long-term need lens.** Today's "Top need" is a one-week snapshot — a team with two Guards on byes could read as a structural Guard need when it's actually a one-week dip. Add a toggle on the Team Needs tab: **This week** (current behavior, schedule-weighted) vs **Rest of season** (per-game projection × remaining games, no game-count weighting). Same math, different denominator. The two will disagree often enough to be informative — a team showing a *persistent* Frontcourt need across both is a real hole; a team that's only weak this week is just streaming.
- ~~**Streamer vs. anchor pickup distinction.**~~ **Shipped in P3** (2026-07-16) as `lineups.tag_target`, with one correction: the rate-based definition this item proposed ("high projected this week but low season per-game") can't split anything, because every claimable free agent rates 18–23/g — the FA median tags them all anchors, the rostered median tags them all streamers. Rate doesn't vary among players worth claiming; **schedule** does. Shipped as: streamer = heavy slate now, ≤2 games next week. Splits the real pool 16/2/12. See spec §10.3. The "Hold value" line in the player modal is not built — fold into P5 if still wanted.
- **Need persistence indicator on team cards.** Show a small streak chip on each team card: "G-need: 3 wks" or "FC-need: 1 wk." Pulls from the daily snapshots in `data/raw/` to detect which top needs are recurring vs transient. A 3+ week need signals it's a real structural hole worth trading for, not a passing slump.
- **Lookback sparkline on team cards.** Tiny inline sparkline (8 weekly points) under each bucket bar showing gap-vs-league over the last 8 weeks. Cheap visual: lets the user see a need *trending up* (getting worse) vs *resolving* (improving). Drives the "drop / hold / acquire" decision better than a single snapshot.
- **Matchup-aware top need.** "Top need" today ignores who the team is playing this week. A team strong at G facing an opponent stacked at G should see Guard-quality picks promoted *for that week* even if the bucket math says they're fine season-wide. Surfaces in a per-matchup card on the Team Needs tab — H2H positional gap vs the assigned opponent, refreshed each matchup period.
- **Full per-player game log inside the player modal.** Modal currently shows projections + this week. Add a 10-game rolling table (date, opponent, pts, min, key stats) once we have a source. ESPN's player splits endpoint is the obvious target; basketball-reference.com is the public fallback.
- **Cumulative cross-team transaction history per player.** Player modal currently only shows the recent (last 50) transaction window. Ship `data/history/transactions.jsonl` to `docs/data/` (or a slimmed version) so the modal can show every move involving the player across the whole season.
- **Player ownership trend in the modal.** Sparkline of `percent_owned` over the last 14 days, sourced from the daily raw snapshots. Fast-rising % owned is a leading indicator of "claim them now."
- **Projection sources beyond ESPN.** Pull projections from wnba.com, Yahoo Sports, and basketball-reference.com to triangulate ESPN's numbers. Reduces single-source bias on waiver-target ranking.
- ~~**Twitter / X signal layer (paid API).**~~ Superseded by the session-cookie approach shipped in Low — no paid keys needed.
- **Reddit r/wnba signal.** Public JSON endpoint (`reddit.com/r/wnba/new.json`) is no-auth. Surface posts flaired "Injury" / "News" / "Game Thread" and match to players by name. Lower precedence than ESPN's official feed.
- **Surname-collision guard for the step 5b-i social-signal dump.** `build_state.py:_detect_social_return_signals` already fixed bare-surname cross-contamination (2026-07-26, `surname_counts` uniqueness check) for return-signal detection, but whatever matches Reddit/news/social posts to a `player_id` for the manual AI-summary authoring dump (step 5b-i in REFRESH.md) doesn't share that guard — found `player_id 3934218` (Megan DiLeo) surfacing a Reddit post actually about "Megan Gustafson" marrying a "David DiLeo" (2026-08-03, see issues.md). Read whichever matcher powers the 5b-i dump and either apply the same uniqueness check or point it at `build_state.py`'s existing matcher instead of a duplicate implementation.
- **My team mode.** Lets the user designate "their" team (via team ID) and gets a daily personalized brief: top need this week, who to drop, who to bid, projected matchup margin.
- **Trade analyzer.** Given two rosters, project the weekly H2H point swing if they traded specific players.
- **Matchup preview cards.** For the upcoming week, show each opposing team's projected output vs. ours by position.

- **TikTok scraper — player highlights and community posts.** TikTok has no public API; the approach is Chrome scraping via the same pattern as Twitter/X. Use `mcp__Claude_in_Chrome` to navigate `https://www.tiktok.com/search/video?q=<player+name>` per top-15 target, extract video metadata (caption, author handle, likes, view count, publish date) into `tiktok_raw.json`. Matching to players: same name-lookup as Twitter (exact full name in caption, unique surname with word boundary). Render in the player modal social feed as `source: "tiktok"` with a `@handle` label. Storage format: `[{title/caption, url, username, published_at, view_count, like_count}]`. Key gotcha: TikTok aggressively redirects to the app on mobile UA — keep desktop UA. CSP will block `fetch()` exfiltration just like X; use the localStorage accumulation + two-phase extraction pattern. Separate sweep for general `#wnba` + `#wnbafantasy` hashtags. **Dependency:** user must be logged into TikTok in Chrome; add to the skill's pre-flight checklist alongside the Twitter login check. *(Priority: Low until TikTok proves higher signal/noise than Twitter for WNBA fantasy.)*
