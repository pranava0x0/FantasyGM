How "top need" is calculated

For each active roster slot (the 9 non-bench / non-IR slots in WNBA fantasy: 2 G + 3 F + 1 F/C + 3 UTIL), we estimate the player's points this week as per-game projection × games this week. Players are bucketed by their default ESPN position (1 = G, 2 = F, 3 = C). Bucket totals per team are compared to the league average for that bucket. Top need is whichever bucket — Guards (G) or combined Frontcourt (F + C) — has the largest negative gap, i.e. the biggest upgrade opportunity.

F and C are bucketed together because WNBA fantasy has shared F/C lineup slots — a team without a pure Center isn't structurally short there if they fill slot 5 with a Forward.

Note: the comparison is on total weekly production, not per-player average. A team with three high-scoring Guards may still show its top need as G if other teams have four or five Guards contributing comparable totals. The Waiver Targets tab boosts picks at the team's top-need bucket and penalizes picks at saturated positions (≥ 5 active Gs or ≥ 7 active F/Cs).
